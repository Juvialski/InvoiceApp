## Comparator observations

- [Stantec — Water](https://www.stantec.com/en/markets/water): a clear Water heading and compact navigation frame a featured project image and a short project descriptor. Service details and a client contact path follow below the opening view.
- [Tetra Tech — One Water Solutions](https://www.tetratech.com/solutions/one-water/solutions/): the opening view pairs a concise service title with a real field/work image. Capability and project detail appears further down the page.
- [AECOM — Water](https://www.aecom.com/en-ca/markets/water/): one sector heading and a full-width field image establish the topic before the service list and deeper explanations.
- [Black & Veatch — Water](https://www.bv.com/en-US/what-we-do/water): a dark, image-led water hero uses a short explanation and a direct contact action, then separates capabilities, audience context, and project references into distinct sections.

## Patterns applied

All four make water engineering apparent immediately, keep the first explanation concise, use simple service navigation, and give contact a visible path. Their named projects and facility/field photos work as proof because they are presented as specific real work. Their scale metrics and service taxonomies depend on company-specific evidence and do not transfer to Hydroqualisense.

The production page therefore uses the existing company mark, an editorial engineering headline, only the confirmed themes of water treatment, water management, and related engineering projects, and an abstract line illustration instead of an unverified facility image. The experience section describes project context without inventing a case study. Public project references and contact details remain explicitly pending company confirmation. Client Portal stays visible as a secondary path.

The QA page, `/demo` entry, and synthetic workspace shell use a separate neutral software-showcase descriptor, capability navigation, a synthetic-data disclosure, and an isolated demo-workspace action. The sample workspace uses an explicitly generic sample-company name instead of attaching invented invoice identity/address details to Hydroqualisense Solutions Corp. It does not use the Hydroqualisense company mark as a software-vendor identity.
